# M.I.R.D.A. philosophy

M.I.R.D.A. — Mira’s Intellectual Resource Development Approach — expresses the project’s culture of curiosity, experimentation, learning, remixing, constructive challenge, and open intellectual exchange.

It welcomes feedback, corrections, improvements, forks, contributions, and unconventional ideas. Credit is appreciated, intellectual courage is encouraged, and contributors are invited to question assumptions rather than preserve them by habit.
